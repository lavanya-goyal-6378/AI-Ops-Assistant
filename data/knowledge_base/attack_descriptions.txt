NETWORK ATTACK DESCRIPTIONS

DDoS: Very high PPS from many source MACs to one destination.
  Features: high fwd_pkt_count, low IAT, max FloodScore
  Response: Block source MACs via Redis TTL. Alert NOC.
 
DoS: High PPS from single source, high SYN count.
  Features: high SYN count, low flow duration, high byte rate
  Response: Block source IP. Apply rate limit.
 
PortScan: Many short flows from one source to many destination ports.
  Features: very short duration, low byte count, high flow count
  Response: Flag source MAC. Apply stricter TTL.
 
CAM Table Overflow: Attacker sends packets from thousands of spoofed
MACs to fill the switch table, forcing it to flood all traffic.
  Features: very high new_mac_rate, mac_fill near 1.0
  Response: Lower new_mac_rate threshold. Apply strict eviction.
